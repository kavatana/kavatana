# Evidence before a comparison

A source criterion supplies one constraint, not a complete clinical policy.

NASA-STD-3001 Volume 1 Revision C, requirement 4003, specifies retaining at least 80% of pre-mission aerobic capacity. Sections 4001 and 4002 address separate mission-dependent EVA capacity requirements. A relative comparison alone cannot establish overall fitness or clearance. [W01]

| Gate | Evidence required for this episode |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | Same operational subject and namespace; no inferred joins between unrelated studies. |
| Baseline | Explicitly selected pre-mission assessment revision, with selector, rationale and review status. Do not silently pick the largest or most recent value. |
| Quantity and unit | Same defined aerobic-capacity quantity; verified conversion only. Preserve raw values and source unit strings. |
| Method and conditions | Method/protocol versions, direct or indirect assessment, relevant conditions and a reviewed comparability decision. |
| Time and phase | Assessment time/interval, original expression, mission phase and clock basis. Relative time may remain relative. |
| Quality and uncertainty | Accepted source quality; missing uncertainty remains unknown. Do not invent confidence bounds or a healthy classification. |
| Applicable release | Exact standard revision/section, reviewed mapping, calculation version and mission-policy scope. |

The team must set any assessment-age or follow-up scheduling policy with a reviewer; NASA 4003 is not used to invent a recency deadline. When a required policy is missing, show Needs review rather than selecting a convenient time window.

Mission-specific medical operations and technical requirements are separate inputs. NASA describes CHOC and medical requirements documents in 6008/6009; this prototype has not obtained a real mission-specific care plan. [W02]

## Coverage beyond the first path

The indicator catalog preserves sleep, cognition, mood, immune/lab, bone, environmental, radiation and imaging needs. Their clinical interpretation remains outside the first completed path. The interface must say Not assessed for these domains, not imply that a single fitness result establishes overall health.

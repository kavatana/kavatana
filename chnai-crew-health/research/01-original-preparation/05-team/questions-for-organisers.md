# Questions for the organisers

For the Local Lead, [local event lead], in the Telegram group or at the Pre-Event Meetup on
18 October. Write the answers here.

## Getting in

1. Is there a shortlist of teams for the hackathon? What are the criteria, and when is it
   announced?
2. Is there a team form on challenge understanding and motivation? Where is it, and when
   is it due?
3. We filled the Pre-Event Meetup form after its 20 September deadline. Are we on the list?
4. Can a team add members after the shortlist, up to six?

## Preparing

5. What preparation is allowed before 14 November? Reading the challenge resources,
   setting up tools and accounts, discussing ideas?
6. May a team use its own open-source starter code if it is public and listed?

## The weekend

7. Where is the venue? Is it open overnight on Saturday?
8. How good is the internet, and may teams use their own hotspots?
9. Are there power sockets at every table?
10. Are meals provided?

## Local judging

11. How long is the pitch, and how long are questions?
12. Is it a live demo, slides, or the 30-second video?
13. In which language?
14. What is submitted at 2:30 PM, and where?
15. What are the local criteria and their weights? How does the on-time bonus point work?
16. How many Global Nominees will Phnom Penh select?
17. Who are the judges, and what are their backgrounds?

## Mentors

18. Is there a mentor with a medical or life-science background?
19. Is there a mentor who has taken part in Space Apps before?

# Recruiting

The team is two engineers. A team of four or five with different skills scores higher on
Validity and Presentation and can sleep.

## Who to find

| Role | Why | Where to look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Health or life science:** a medical, nursing, pharmacy, biology or public-health student | Checks that the product's claims are medically sound; answers the judges' medical questions | University of Health Sciences, International University, University of Puthisastra; the event's Telegram group; the 18 October meetup |
| **Designer** | One view finished to a high standard; the slides | Design students; the "Seeking Members" lists on the site |
| **Storyteller and video** | The 30-second demo, the pitch script, the project page text | Media and communication students |
| A third engineer, optional | Data fetching and tests while the others build | Only after the three above |

## Rules to remember

- At most six members.
- Everyone must be registered for the **Phnom Penh** event, and not on the waitlist.
- Everyone must be on the team's Members tab before submission closes.
- A person can have only one pending join request at a time.
- No one under 18 may appear in the demo or on the team page photo.

## A message to post in the Telegram group

```
Hi everyone. We are CHNAI LAB, two software engineers (full-stack and AI) working on "Create Health Monitoring Software for Astronauts on Space Missions". We are looking for 2 or 3 teammates: someone studying medicine, nursing, pharmacy or biology; a designer; and someone who likes writing and video. No coding needed for these roles. If you are registered for Phnom Penh and interested, message me here. We will also be at the meetup on 18 October.
```

## What to ask a candidate

1. Are you registered for the Phnom Penh event on the official site?
2. Can you be there in person on both days, for the whole schedule?
3. What would you most like to do on the team?

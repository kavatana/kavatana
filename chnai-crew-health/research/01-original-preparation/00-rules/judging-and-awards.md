# Judging and awards

The 2026 Judging & Awards Guide is published on **13 November**. What follows is the 2025
guide plus what the Phnom Penh organisers have said. Re-read on 13 November.

## The rounds

| Round | Who decides | What they look at | What gets through |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0. Phnom Penh shortlist | Local organisers | The team's understanding of its challenge and its commitment | A place at the event |
| 1. Local judging | Phnom Penh judges, Sunday 3:00 PM | The live pitch and the project | Global Nominees: about one per ten projects submitted |
| 2. Global judging | NASA and partner-agency experts | The project page only | Global Finalists and Honorable Mentions (December) |
| 3. Executive judging | Senior judges | The finalists | Ten Global Winners (January 2027) |

### Round 0: the Phnom Penh shortlist

This is not on the official site. It comes from the organisers' orientation on 26 September
(a rough machine transcript of the Khmer recording) and from a Cambodianess article, which
says shortlisted applicants are invited to the 18 October meetup.

What the recording says, as far as it can be read:

- The organisers shortlist teams. They said they use two criteria, "to determine whether
  the team is fully aware of what they are going to do and their commitment".
- A form in which the team writes its understanding of the challenge and its motivation
  was discussed. Teams submit it to be shortlisted.
- Someone asked whether an app or a website is better. The answer was that the project is
  checked against the objectives in the challenge details, so either is fine.

**Confirm all three with the Local Lead.** Until then, act as if they are true:
`../05-team/` has the answers ready.

### Round 1: local judging

- Phnom Penh has 30 teams on 1 October and 285 people signed up. If 40 to 60 projects are
  submitted, expect roughly four to six Global Nominees.
- Four other Phnom Penh teams chose our challenge: Big Boss, Larpers, Space Mols, TerraX.
  Local judges compare all projects together, across all challenges.
- The local schedule gives a bonus point for submitting on time (2:30 PM Sunday).
- There is a People's Choice vote at 4:30 PM.
- Pitch length, format and local weighting are not published. Ask on 18 October.

### Rounds 2 and 3: global

- 2025 numbers: 114,000+ participants, 551 local events, 11,500+ projects, 1,290+ Global
  Nominees, 10 winners.
- This challenge already has **687 teams** worldwide (1 October). It is one of the crowded
  ones, so the project page has to stand out in the first few lines.
- "The majority of judges' evaluation will be based on your project submission." Global
  judges never see the live pitch.

## The five criteria (2025: each scored 1 to 5)

| Criterion | The question judges ask | What earns the score |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Impact** | How much does it matter, and to whom? | A real problem, a clear user, a credible path to being used |
| **Creativity** | Is it new? | An approach other teams did not take |
| **Validity** | Is it scientifically sound and could it work? | Correct science, honest limits, something that runs |
| **Relevance** | Does it answer this challenge, and is NASA data central to it? | NASA data "integral… rather than a superficial addition" |
| **Presentation** | Is it told well? | A clear story in the demo and on the page |

## The ten Global Awards

Best Use of Science, Best Use of Data, Best Use of Technology, Galactic Impact, Best
Mission Concept, Most Inspirational, Best Storytelling, Global Connection, Art &
Technology, Local Impact.

For health monitoring software, the natural targets are **Best Use of Technology**, **Best
Use of Science** and **Galactic Impact**.

## What the 2025 winners had in common

Read their pages before the weekend: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2025/awards/global-winners/

- One sentence says what the thing is.
- It works, and the page links to it.
- NASA data is the engine, and the page names each dataset.
- Small teams win. "SpaceGenes+" (Best Use of Science) started as one UX designer.

## The orientation of 26 September

The organisers shared a folder with the slides and a 53-minute recording, mostly in Khmer.

- Slides: `reference/phnom-penh-orientation-2026-09-26.pdf`. They are the official Space
  Apps deck on team formation and challenges, presented by the Local Lead.
- Recording: transcribed by machine on 1 October. The transcript is rough, with many
  errors, so the points below are what could be made out and need confirming.

What the recording appears to say:

1. Teams will be **shortlisted**. Two criteria were named in English: whether the team "is
   fully aware of what they are going to do", and "their commitment".
2. The organisers want everyone to know the criteria "and how we are going to select, so
   it's fair".
3. A participant asked about a form. The reply mentioned a form in which the team fills in
   its challenge understanding and motivation, to be submitted for shortlisting.
4. Asked whether an app or a website is better, the reply was that projects are checked
   against the objectives in the challenge details, so either is acceptable.
5. The session ended with breakout rooms for meeting people and recruiting.

A second machine transcript with a different Khmer model was no better. It does place the
words "shortlisting process" at about minute 47 of the recording. The quickest way to
settle points 1 to 3 is for a team member to listen from minute 46 to minute 50
(`Orientation.mp4` in the organisers' folder, linked in `../sources.md`).

# Rules and eligibility

Checked 1 October 2026. Sources are in `../sources.md`.

## Before 14 November, 9:00 AM

### Allowed

- Register, pick the Phnom Penh local event, create or join a team, choose the challenge.
- Read the challenge statement and its resources. The Team Formation Guide says: "We
  encourage you to review each challenge statement and select one for your team project
  *before* the hackathon starts."
- Ask questions about the challenge and its resources on Space Apps Connect, "in the week
  prior to and during the hackathon".
- Learn the science, learn where the data is and how to fetch it, learn and practise tools.
- Recruit teammates, agree on roles, set up laptops and accounts.
- Go to the Pre-Event Meetup on 18 October.

### Not allowed

- Beginning work on the project. The rule does not list what counts, so we treat all of
  these as project work and do none of them early: code for the solution, screens or
  mockups of it, data prepared specially for it, the slides, the video, the project text.

### Not defined by the rules

Talking about ideas. Nobody can stop a team from thinking. We keep ideas in our heads and in
conversation, write no specification, and ask the Local Lead where the line is.

## Team rules

- One to six members. Four or five is the practical size.
- Every member must be registered for the **same local event** (Phnom Penh), not on the
  waitlist, and listed on the team's Members tab before submission closes.
- One team per person, one challenge per team.
- The Team Owner creates the team, approves join requests, and can change the challenge.
  The owner's local event sets the team's event and its submission time zone.
- The owner must be a confirmed participant at the local event for the project to be
  eligible for local awards.
- A person can have only one pending join request at a time.
- The site has no chat. The team's contact details on the About tab are how people reach us.
- Do not put private details such as age or home address on the team page.

## During the hackathon

- **Use NASA data.** "All projects must use NASA data." It is also the condition for any
  Global Award. Judges look for data that is central to the project, not decoration.
- **List every source**, including open-source libraries and partner-agency data.
- **Disclose AI use.** AI tools are allowed. AI images and video need a visible
  "AI-generated" mark. AI text, code, audio and data must be acknowledged in writing.
- **No NASA logos or branding** in anything generated or designed by the team.
- **Open source.** The team's original work is released under an open licence. If none is
  named, Apache 2.0 applies. Third-party material must be free to use or OSI-licensed.
- **No personal data** of real people, and no faces or voices of anyone under 18.
- **No commercial promotion.** The team name can be CHNAI LAB. The team page and the
  project must not advertise products or services.
- **English** for slides and for video subtitles.
- **Public links, no login** for the demo and the final project.
- **Demo limit:** a video of at most 30 seconds, or at most 7 slides including the title
  slide. Going over makes the project ineligible. (2025 rule; confirm on 13 November.)
- **Deadline:** Sunday 15 November, 11:59 PM Phnom Penh time, on the team's project page.
  Phnom Penh also has its own pitch deadline at 2:30 PM that day.

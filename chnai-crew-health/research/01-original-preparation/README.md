# Nasa Project

CHNAI LAB's preparation for the **2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge**, Phnom Penh local event.

- **Team:** CHNAI LAB ([team lead], [teammate]; room for up to four more): https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/find-a-team/chnai-lab/
- **Challenge:** Create Health Monitoring Software for Astronauts on Space Missions
- **Hackathon:** Saturday 14 and Sunday 15 November 2026, in person in Phnom Penh

## The one rule that shapes this folder

> "Teams **are not allowed** to begin working on the challenges prior to the hackathon."
> (Participant FAQs, 23 September 2026)

> "participants should NOT begin working on their project until the hackathon begins on
> November 14, 2026 (9:00 a.m. local time) to ensure a fair and equitable process."
> (Team Formation Guide)

So this folder holds **study and readiness only**: the rules, the science, the data sources,
the tools we know how to use, and the team. It holds **no project work**: no code for the
solution, no screens or mockups of it, no slides, no video, no project text. All of that
starts at 9:00 AM on 14 November and goes in a new `project/` folder created that morning.

The rule does not define "working on the project". Our reading is the careful one. Ask the
Local Lead at the 18 October meetup what preparation is allowed
(`05-team/questions-for-organisers.md`).

# How a team gets through each round

Four gates. Each one is passed for different reasons, so each has its own plan.

## Gate 0: the Phnom Penh shortlist (now to mid-October)

**Who decides:** the local organisers. **On what:** whether the team understands its
challenge and is committed. This comes from the organisers' own recording; confirm it.

What shows understanding and commitment:

- [x] The team page exists, with a real description, a contact, and the challenge chosen. (1 October)
- [x] Both members are registered for Phnom Penh on the official site and on the team page. (1 October)
- [ ] Both members have filled the Pre-Event Meetup form. Its deadline was 20 September and
      it was still open on 1 October: fill it today and tell the Local Lead.
- [ ] Both are in the Telegram group and answer when the organisers ask something.
- [ ] If a team form on "challenge understanding and motivation" is sent, answer it the same
      day. A draft is in `../05-team/pre-event-form-answers.md`.
- [ ] Both attend on 18 October, on time, and stay for the whole day.

## Gate 1: local judging (Sunday 15 November, 3:00 PM)

**Who decides:** Phnom Penh judges. **On what:** a live pitch. **Passing:** roughly the
top one in ten becomes a Global Nominee. With 40 to 60 projects, that is four to six teams.

What decides it in the room:

1. **A working product, shown live.** Most teams will show slides about an idea.
2. **One sentence the judges can repeat.** If they cannot say what it is, they cannot vote for it.
3. **NASA's data and NASA's limits on the screen**, named. This is Relevance and Validity
   together, and it is what separates this project from four others on the same challenge.
4. **On time.** Submit the pitch before 2:30 PM for the bonus point.
5. **Answers.** Judges ask "is this real data?" and "would an astronaut use this?". Have
   the honest answer ready for both.
6. **The room.** There is a People's Choice vote at 4:30 PM.

## Gate 2: global judging (after 15 November)

**Who decides:** NASA and partner-agency experts. **On what:** the project page and
nothing else. **Passing:** Global Finalist or Honorable Mention, announced in December.

What decides it on the page:

1. **The first three lines of the summary.** Over a thousand projects on this challenge.
2. **The 30-second demo.** It shows the product working, not people talking.
3. **The NASA Data field.** Each dataset named, with what it does in the product.
4. **The link works**, with no login, on the judge's own laptop, weeks later.
5. **Honesty.** What is real, what is simulated, what the limits are.

Sunday evening, from the end of the local ceremony until 9:00 PM, is for this page.

## Gate 3: the ten Global Awards (January 2027)

Chosen from the finalists by senior judges. The fitting awards:

| Award | What it takes |
| --- | --- |
| Best Use of Technology | The technology itself is the answer to the mission's constraint |
| Best Use of Science | Every decision in the product is traceable to NASA's science |
| Galactic Impact | It could change how crews, or people on Earth, look after their health |

## What the five criteria need from this challenge

| Criterion | What scores |
| --- | --- |
| Impact | The astronaut can act alone. A stated benefit for people on Earth far from a doctor, with evidence |
| Creativity | An approach other than the dashboard-plus-chatbot that most teams will build |
| Validity | Limits from NASA-STD-3001 with their IDs. Tested rules. Simulated data labelled. A health or life-science teammate who checked it |
| Relevance | Covers the challenge's own words: gathers, evaluate, act. NASA data is the engine |
| Presentation | One sentence, one path, 30 seconds |

## What not to do

- Do not start building early. Starting before 9:00 AM on 14 November breaks the rule and
  puts the project's eligibility at risk, and the repository's history shows when work began.
- Do not present simulated data as real.
- Do not let a language model give medical advice in the demo.
- Do not use the NASA logo.
- Do not build five features. Build one path, finished.
- Do not advertise CHNAI LAB's products on the team page or the project page.

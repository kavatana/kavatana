# Earth side: research, archives and analogs

Where the evidence lives. Use it to make every claim in the project traceable.

## NASA's risk list

The Human Research Program tracks about thirty human health risks, each with an evidence
report. The ones this challenge touches:

| Group | Risks |
| --- | --- |
| Body | Immune response; cardiovascular; bone; muscle and aerobic fitness; sensorimotor; eyes (SANS); food and nutrition; microbes; decompression sickness; low oxygen; spacewalk injury |
| Mind and team | Behavioural and psychiatric conditions; team performance; sleep loss and work schedules |
| Radiation | Cancer; effects on the heart and the nervous system |
| Medical care | Not enough medical capability on board for the distance |

- The list: https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/risks/
- Each risk with its evidence, gaps and studies: https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/Risks/

When a feature exists because of a risk, name the risk and link its page.

## Analogs: missions simulated on Earth

Analogs produce data on isolation, confinement, workload and sleep from ordinary healthy
people, which is easier to publish than astronaut data.

| Analog | What it imitates |
| --- | --- |
| HERA, at Johnson Space Center | Weeks of isolation and confinement with a small crew |
| CHAPEA, at Johnson Space Center | A year on a simulated Mars surface, including message delay |
| Bed rest studies | The body's response to weightlessness: bone, muscle, heart |
| Antarctic stations | Months of isolation, darkness and distance |

Their datasets are found through the NASA Life Sciences Portal. **Not yet searched.**

## The Inspiration4 datasets

A three-day orbital flight in 2021 with four private crew members, who agreed to make their
biomedical data open. In OSDR on 1 October 2026:

| ID | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| OSD-569 | Whole blood: blood counts from a haematology analyser, gene activity, genome |
| OSD-570 | Immune cells, cell by cell |
| OSD-571 | Blood plasma: proteins, metabolites |
| OSD-572 | Mouth, nose and skin microbes |
| OSD-573 | Microbes on the capsule's surfaces |
| OSD-574 | Skin biopsies |
| OSD-630 | Gut microbes |
| OSD-687 | T-cell markers |

Limits to state whenever it is used: four people, three days, low Earth orbit. It shows
that real human spaceflight health data is open. It does not describe a Mars mission.

## Earth benefit

Space Apps asks for tools that help "on Earth and in space". Monitoring health where no
doctor is near also describes a rural clinic, a ship, or a remote work site. If the team
claims an Earth benefit, it needs its own evidence, not an assertion.

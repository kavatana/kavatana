# Earth side: the person before and after

A reading in space is judged against the same person on Earth. NASA's limits are mostly
written that way: "80% of baseline", "95% of pre-mission values".

## Before launch: the baseline

| Measured on Earth | Why | The limit that later uses it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VO2max | Fitness before deconditioning | V1 4003: stay at or above 80% of it |
| Muscle strength (deadlift, bench press) | Strength before loss | V1 4023 minimums; V1 4024: stay at or above 80% |
| Bone density by DXA, hip and spine | Bone before loss | V1 4026; V1 4027: 95% and 90% |
| Blood counts and immune markers | The person's normal | V1 4015: within the healthy population's range |
| Nutritional status | Deficiencies fixed before flight | V1 4019 |
| Cognitive test scores | The person's normal speed and accuracy | V1 4011 |
| Balance and coordination | The person's normal | V1 4005 |
| Eye imaging and vision | For spotting SANS later | V1 3003 m |
| Radiation dose from earlier flights | Counts toward the career limit | V1 4030: 600 mSv |
| Medical selection and yearly recertification | Fit to fly | V1 3001 |

## During the mission: the ground team

| Who | Role | Limits on them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flight surgeon | The crew's doctor; holds private medical conferences | Message delay and blackouts |
| Behavioural health team | Private psychological conferences; support for families | The same |
| Mission control | Watches the vehicle and the schedule | The same |

Medical communication is private (V1 6002, 6003), and medical data is sent privately
(V1 3004 s).

## After landing: recovery and the long term

| What | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Fitness returns to the pre-mission value | V1 4004 |
| Strength returns to baseline | V1 4025 |
| Bone density returns to baseline | V1 4028; not all bone lost is regained |
| Blood and immune values return to baseline | V1 4018 |
| Thinking and behavioural health return to baseline | V1 4012, V1 4013 |
| Lifelong monitoring "in a standardized manner" | V1 3018 |

## What is open and what is not

| Data | Open? |
| --- | --- |
| The limits and the reasoning behind them | Yes: NASA-STD-3001 |
| Average changes seen in astronauts | Yes: HRP evidence reports and published papers |
| A named astronaut's baseline or recovery | No: private medical data |
| The Inspiration4 crew's before, during and after measurements | Yes: OSDR, with the crew's consent |

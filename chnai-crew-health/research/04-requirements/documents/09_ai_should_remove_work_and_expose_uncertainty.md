# AI should remove work and expose uncertainty

09 / Bounded AI requirements

Recent NASA CMO-DA work supports studying agentic assistance and local inference. The 2026 Red Hat account describes testing on a terrestrial twin of Spaceborne Computer hardware; it does not establish a flight-qualified medical system. The prototype should learn from that direction without claiming equivalent validation.

Good initial model tasks are evidence retrieval, plain-language explanation, missing-context questions and draft handoffs. Units, ratios, time windows, access rules, record updates and state transitions remain governed by reviewed deterministic logic. The model receives narrowly scoped evidence, not the entire research archive by default.

Every factual output must trace to source sections and accepted record revisions. A validation layer rejects unsupported claims and unauthorized tool arguments. Imported documents are untrusted content, not instructions. The user can see the active mode, stop a task and continue through a useful non-model path.

Fine-tuning is a decision gate, not an automatic sign of sophistication. It needs a task-specific shortfall, permitted labeled examples, leakage-resistant held-out evaluation and an improvement over fixed templates and retrieval-based baselines. The current small, mixed cohorts do not justify training an astronaut diagnostic model.

- Measure usefulness and errors separately from fluent writing.
- No model-generated treatment advice in the demo.
- Pin model, prompt, evidence and tool-policy versions.
- Cloud inference may be compared, but the core review must survive its absence.

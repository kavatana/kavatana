# From evidence to engineering decisions

Research and requirements baseline | v0.1 | 19 October 2026

CHNAI LAB can now begin a structured comparison of client, API, storage, inference and automation approaches. The collection contains concrete file formats, data-quality failures, mission constraints and proposed verification cases. This is readiness to compare technologies, not a completed technology selection or a validated medical product.

Proposed product sentence: A private onboard assistant that assembles health evidence, explains what needs review, and keeps the next authorized action moving when Earth is delayed.

The current release contains 84 proposed requirements, 58 unexecuted acceptance cases, 38 candidate indicators, 10 role definitions, 15 dataset access cards and 19 source-to-field mappings. The source index has 132 entries, including earlier research and access notes; this is not a count of independent datasets or cohorts.

The strongest implementation direction is one complete onboard review episode. Data ingestion, missingness, interpretation, human authority, follow-up and delayed handoff should form one demonstrable path. All other health domains need honest availability states.

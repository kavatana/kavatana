# NASA limits require context

08 / Applicability and clinical boundaries

The limits registry records source revision, requirement ID, quantity, averaging window, baseline and conditions for use. The numbers are not copied into a generic red/green dashboard. For example, the CO2 requirement concerns an average one-hour partial pressure; a raw ppm reading is a different quantity.

Aerobic-capacity retention and site-specific bone-density retention depend on comparable pre-mission measurements. Exercise completion and molecular markers may inform monitoring but do not directly measure BMD. Career effective radiation dose is not an acute alarm threshold on a particle detector.

NASA standards describe program and mission requirements. This project derives testable software behaviors from relevant clauses; it does not claim certification or complete applicability to an unspecified mission. A reviewer must approve each health interpretation and next action.

Current V1 includes ground clearance before each EVA. Deep-space autonomy research does not justify inventing an autonomous clearance feature. The prototype excludes prescribing, unrestricted diagnosis, flight clearance and spacecraft control.

- Immune, skeletal, cardiovascular and behavioral domains remain visible with measured, indirect or unavailable evidence states.
- Core and peripheral skin temperature are distinct. NASA's 2026 temperature-monitoring call describes continuing research needs.
- Behavioral sensing must not be presented as diagnosis or crew surveillance.

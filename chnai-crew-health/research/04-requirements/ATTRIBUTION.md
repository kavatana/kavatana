# Source attribution and reuse notes

## ACTES cycle exercise v1.0.0

Chabert, C., Mongin, D., Hermand, E., Collado, A., and Hue, O. (2022). *Cardiorespiratory measurement from graded cycloergometer exercise testing*, version 1.0.0. PhysioNet. https://doi.org/10.13026/2qs3-kh43

License observed: Open Data Commons Attribution License v1.0. The original bundled license is in `data/cycle-exercise/LICENSE.txt`; the retrieved current license page is indexed as R018. Dataset files are preserved unmodified. Derived format profiles and quality findings are CHNAI LAB research preparation. They do not change the original measurements.

The dataset page additionally requests attribution to Mongin, D., Garcia Romero, J., and Alvero Cruz, J. R. (2021), *Treadmill Maximal Exercise Tests from the Exercise Physiology and Human Performance Lab of the University of Malaga*, version 1.0.1, https://doi.org/10.13026/7ezk-j442 and links https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2021.09.23.21263943v1.full. This citation is retained as requested by the page, without treating the treadmill measurements as acquired or their reuse rights as resolved.

## Sleep-EDF Expanded v1.0.0

Sleep-EDF Database Expanded, version 1.0.0. PhysioNet. https://physionet.org/content/sleep-edfx/1.0.0/

Original publication requested by the dataset: Kemp, B., Zwinderman, A. H., Tuk, B., Kamphuisen, H. A. C., and Oberye, J. J. L. (2000). Analysis of a sleep-dependent neuronal feedback loop: the slow-wave microcontinuity of the EEG. *IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering*, 47(9), 1185-1194. https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/867928/

License observed: Open Data Commons Attribution License v1.0, retained in source snapshot R016. One PSG and its annotation companion are preserved unmodified, with publisher hashes and subject metadata. Their original local recovery date is not claimed as their original download date. The profile output is a derived engineering analysis, not a new clinical dataset.

## NASA and other primary materials

NASA standards, NTRS documents, OSDR study metadata, CSA device descriptions, W3C recommendations, HL7 and EDF specifications, NIST guidance and vendor documentation are attributed in the Sources register. Preserve source URL, revision and retrieval status. Follow per-file terms and third-party notices; government hosting alone does not establish every third-party right.

OSD-51, OSD-195 and OSD-198 ISA metadata preserve their own study and publication references. Inspiration4 and earlier acquired research assets remain in the original collection with their original provenance. Human research data must not be reidentified or silently merged into operational crew records.

## Four examples of requirement applicability

These are paraphrases for research. The exact standard, revision, mission applicability and clinical review govern implementation.

| Requirement | Published constraint | Why it is not a generic alarm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NASA-STD-3001 V1 Rev C, 4003 | In-mission aerobic capacity at least 80% of pre-mission capacity | Needs a comparable individual baseline and direct/indirect assessment |
| V1 Rev C, 4027 | BMD at least 95% of pre-mission hip/spine values and 90% for femoral neck | Site-specific bone outcome; exercise adherence does not measure it |
| V1 Rev C, 4030 | Career effective spaceflight radiation dose below 600 mSv | Not an acute storm limit and not interchangeable with instrument absorbed dose |
| V2 Rev F, 6004 | Average one-hour nominal cabin CO2 partial pressure no more than 3 mmHg | Requires partial pressure and a matching time window; not a raw ppm instant threshold |

Sources: retained NASA-STD-3001 PDFs in 02_Mission_and_Standards; source IDs S008 and S009.

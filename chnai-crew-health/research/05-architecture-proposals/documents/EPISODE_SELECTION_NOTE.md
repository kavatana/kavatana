# Why the proposed success path uses a comparable assessment

The 19 October baseline deliberately kept multiple workflow options open. The OSD-569 CBC hemoglobin discrepancy is useful evidence for a data-quality failure branch: preserve the source, quarantine interpretation and request a reviewed mapping. A quarantine demonstration alone does not show the entire gather, evaluate and act objective.

The new architecture therefore recommends evaluating a success path using a **clearly simulated** personal aerobic-capacity baseline and follow-up assessment. NASA-STD-3001 Volume 1, Revision C, requirement 4003 supplies the performance criterion. The software would need method/population/measurement comparability and a health review before describing the comparison as applicable. A proposed permitted action is to request the assigned crew medical role's review and record follow-up, not to prescribe exercise or treatment.

This is a scenario proposal, not a fabricated NASA dataset. It does not convert ACTES youth exercise measurements into astronaut VO2max, resolve their reported unit-scale issue, or imply a wearable directly measures every health domain. Any real NASA research example remains separately labeled and scoped.

The full challenge on 28 October may require another resource or success path. Keep the requirements baseline unchanged until that review. Current decision: architecture can support this path; episode content is still unreviewed. Gate G03 remains open.
